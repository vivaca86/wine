# Model Personas

Last updated: 2026-04-27

These personas are reusable AI ecommerce model identities for product image generation. They should create consistent brand tone across shoes, socks, hosiery, tights, leggings, and later clothing categories.

Important rules:

- Do not imitate or resemble any real celebrity, idol, influencer, or public figure.
- The model should look like a polished ecommerce fashion model, not a fantasy character.
- Product visibility comes before model vanity.
- Skin, posture, hands, feet, ankles, and pose mechanics must remain realistic.
- Use each persona consistently across multiple products when building brand continuity.

## Female Persona A: Petite Maknae Casual Model

### Core Identity

A petite, cute, fresh ecommerce fashion model with K-pop idol maknae-inspired styling. She is best for casual shoes, sneakers, cute socks, flats, loafers, and playful daily styling.

### Visual Profile

- Age impression: around 20 years old.
- Height impression: 158-159 cm.
- Body: petite, slim, balanced proportions.
- Mood: cute, bright, fresh, friendly, slightly shy but confident.
- Face: small soft face, rounded and gentle impression, clear realistic skin.
- Hair: natural black or dark brown; long straight hair, soft waves, or half-up style.
- Makeup: subtle Korean idol-style makeup, clear skin, coral/pink tones, natural gloss.
- Leg/ankle priority: slender ankles, clean calf line, shoes and socks remain easy to see.
- Must remain adult: not childlike, not underage-looking.

### Best Product Categories

- Sneakers.
- Cute socks.
- No-show socks.
- Ankle socks.
- Flats.
- Loafers.
- Casual stockings.
- Daily low heels.
- Casual legwear.

### Best Styling

- Pleated skirt.
- Mini skirt.
- Cropped pants.
- Denim.
- Knit top.
- Cardigan.
- Oversized shirt.
- Casual blouse.
- Soft pastel or clean neutral colors.

### Pose Direction

- One foot forward.
- Toe slightly turned inward for a cute styling cue, only if it does not distort shoe visibility.
- Gentle walking step.
- Seated pose showing ankle/sock/shoe detail.
- Below-knee crop for socks and sneakers.
- Full-body pose where shoes remain visible and not too small.

### Camera Direction

- Avoid over-lengthening legs unnaturally.
- Keep petite proportions believable.
- Use slightly low angle only when shoes need emphasis.
- For socks, use ankle/lower-calf framing.
- For shoes, use one foot forward and lower-frame prominence.

### Prompt Block

```text
A petite young female ecommerce fashion model, around 20 years old appearance, 158-159 cm height impression, polished K-pop idol maknae-inspired styling, cute and fresh visual impression, small soft face, clear realistic skin texture, slim petite proportions, elegant leg line and slender ankles, natural dark brown or black hair, subtle Korean idol-style makeup, bright but believable shopping mall model presence, adult appearance, not childlike, not resembling any real person. The model supports the product and does not steal attention from shoes, socks, or legwear.
```

### Negative Constraints

```text
not childlike, not underage-looking, not resembling any real celebrity or idol, not fantasy-like, not plastic skin, not over-airbrushed, not distorted petite proportions, no cropped feet, no pose that hides the product
```

## Female Persona B: Soft Office Muse

### Core Identity

A soft, elegant, professional ecommerce fashion model for office styling. She is best for pumps, loafers, stockings, tights, slacks, skirts, and clean formal-casual outfits.

### Visual Profile

- Age impression: 24-27 years old.
- Height impression: 164-167 cm.
- Body: slim but not overly thin, balanced and graceful.
- Mood: calm, refined, approachable, elegant, professional.
- Face: oval face, soft eyes, clean skin, calm natural smile.
- Hair: dark brown or black; long straight hair, layered C-curl, or low ponytail.
- Makeup: rose-beige / nude-pink Korean makeup, subtle semi-matte skin, not heavy.
- Leg/ankle priority: clean leg line, graceful ankles, ideal for pumps and hosiery.

### Best Product Categories

- Basic pumps.
- Pointed-toe pumps.
- Kitten heels.
- Loafers.
- Nude sheer pantyhose.
- Black sheer tights.
- Opaque tights.
- Office socks / dress socks.
- Slacks.
- Midi skirts.

### Best Styling

- Blouse.
- Shirt.
- Soft knit.
- Jacket.
- Tailored slacks.
- H-line skirt.
- Midi skirt.
- Minimal handbag.
- Neutral, charcoal, ivory, beige, black, muted rose, navy.

### Pose Direction

- One foot forward in a polished standing pose.
- Gentle walking office pose.
- Subtle leg-cross stance.
- Natural handbag pose.
- Below-knee detail for pumps and hosiery.
- Ankle-down close-up for low-vamp pump, hosiery, and toe detail.

### Camera Direction

- Full-body shots: slightly low angle, head-to-toe, shoes still prominent.
- Below-knee shots: leg line, stockings, and shoe opening emphasized.
- Close-ups: foot/ankle/low-vamp details, hosiery texture, shoe pressure, and leather edge finishing.
- Avoid cold, overly distant, or intimidating posing.

### Prompt Block

```text
A young adult female ecommerce fashion model, 24-27 years old appearance, polished K-pop idol older-sister / Korean office muse styling, elegant and soft professional visual impression, slim balanced proportions, clean leg line, graceful ankles, realistic clear skin texture, dark brown or black layered hair, subtle rose-beige Korean makeup, calm natural smile, sophisticated but approachable shopping mall model presence, not resembling any real person. The model supports pumps, loafers, hosiery, tights, and office styling without overpowering the product.
```

### Negative Constraints

```text
not resembling any real celebrity or idol, not cold or intimidating, not over-airbrushed, not waxy skin, not fantasy-like, no cropped feet, no pose that hides shoes or hosiery, no inconsistent skirt length, no distorted legs or impossible ankle angle
```

## Persona Use Rules

### Keep Product Priority

Always combine persona prompts with product-emphasis language:

```text
The model pose, crop, camera angle, and outfit are chosen to make the product easy to inspect. Product remains the visual priority.
```

### Keep Persona Consistent

When generating a product set:

- Use the same persona across all 8 shots unless intentionally changing models.
- Keep hair, makeup, body proportion, and styling tone consistent.
- Outfit can change by product category but should remain within persona style.
- Do not let full-body shots look like a different person from detail shots.

### Match Persona To Product

- Female Persona A: casual shoes, sneakers, cute socks, daily flats, playful legwear.
- Female Persona B: pumps, loafers, office socks, stockings, tights, slacks, skirts.
