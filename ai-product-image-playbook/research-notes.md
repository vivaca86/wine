# Research Notes

These notes summarize useful visual references and prompt lessons for AI ecommerce fashion images.

## Hosiery Denier and Visibility

- Lower denier means more transparency. 10D-15D nude hosiery should show the skin underneath clearly.
- 15D-20D still reads as sheer but is a little more polished and smoothing.
- The stocking should not erase anatomy. On close-up footwear shots, faint veins, tendons, ankle bones, toe shapes, and skin tone variation can remain visible under the nylon.
- Reinforced toe areas can be slightly denser than the leg area, but should not look like opaque socks unless that is the product.
- Matte hosiery gives a soft controlled finish. Glossy hosiery produces stronger shin/instep highlights.

Prompt terms that worked better:

```text
translucent beige nylon layer over natural skin tone
fine nylon knit fiber texture
skin details remain visible under the sheer layer
faint blue-green veins on the instep and ankle
subtle ankle bones, tendons, toe knuckle shapes, soft toe outlines
subtle matte-satin sheen following the leg curve
slight fabric tension around toes, ball of foot, ankle, and shoe opening
```

Prompt terms to avoid or use carefully:

```text
smooth skin
perfect legs
beauty blur
airbrushed skin
sheer stockings
```

The last one, `sheer stockings`, is not bad by itself, but it is too vague. It must be paired with fiber, transparency, compression, and skin-under-fabric details.

## Pump Creasing and Foot Mechanics

- A pump should not crease randomly.
- In a flat standing pose, creasing may be minimal.
- When the heel lifts and weight moves to the forefoot, the vamp should react around the ball of foot.
- A strong tiptoe pose should show pressure at the ball of foot and toes, with the heel lifted and shadow visible below.
- Creases are usually shallow compression lines, not cracks.
- Sidewall compression or slight bulging near the metatarsal area helps sell the physics.

Prompt terms that worked better:

```text
heel lifted high off the floor
supported mostly by the ball of the foot and toes
vamp creases at the ball-of-foot flex line
shallow horizontal compression wrinkles across the vamp
slight side bulging and compression at the metatarsal area
subtle tension along the shoe opening
realistic shadow under the lifted heel
```

## Skin Under Hosiery

A realistic sheer-stocking leg is a layered material problem:

1. Anatomy layer: skin tone, bones, tendons, veins, toe shapes.
2. Fabric layer: nylon fibers, denier transparency, seam/reinforced areas.
3. Lighting layer: matte/satin sheen and shadows.
4. Contact layer: compression where the foot enters the shoe or bears weight.

Prompts should describe all four layers. If only fabric is described, the result can look like texture pasted over a blurred leg. If only skin is described, the result can look bare-legged.

## Material Detail Prompts By Category

### Smooth Calf Leather

```text
fine natural leather grain, soft curved highlights, clean folded edge finishing, subtle heel counter seam, shallow compression wrinkles only at flex points, not cracked, not melted
```

### Patent Leather

```text
high-gloss reflective patent leather, crisp specular highlights, smooth lacquered surface, reflections following curved toe and heel, no cloudy blur
```

### Suede

```text
short suede nap, directional pile, soft matte texture, subtle color variation where nap changes direction, no plastic smoothness
```

### Mesh Sneaker Panels

```text
fine breathable mesh weave, tiny perforation grid, soft fabric depth, panel seam boundaries, not random noise
```

### Rubber Sole

```text
matte rubber edge, molded sole line, shallow tread hint, crisp outsole edge, realistic floor contact shadow
```

### Ribbed Knit Clothing

```text
fine rib knit texture, vertical rib structure, fabric tension over body curves, soft cotton/wool fibers, realistic sleeve and hem edges
```

### Suiting / Skirt Fabric

```text
charcoal wool suiting texture, fine twill weave, structured drape, clean pressed hem, subtle fabric grain, realistic folds at hip and knee movement
```
