# Prompt Templates

Use these as reusable prompt blocks. Replace bracketed fields before generating.

## Universal Quality Block

```text
Maximum available resolution, 4K-quality ultra realistic Korean ecommerce product photography, made for zoomed product-page inspection. Sharp material detail, realistic lighting, natural shadows, believable body mechanics, no beauty blur, no plastic skin, no fake logos, no text, no labels.
```

## Universal Negative Block

```text
Negative constraints: no cropped shoes, no cropped toes, no cropped heels, no mismatched left and right shoes, no distorted feet, no impossible ankle angle, no warped heel, no melted sole, no fake logo, no text, no labels, no extra limbs, no extra fingers, no muddy skin texture, no waxy doll skin, no smooth blur filter, no random wrinkles.
```

## Hosiery Block: Nude Sheer 10-15D

Use when the stocking must be visibly sheer and transparent.

```text
Hosiery: nude sheer 10-15 denier pantyhose, very transparent and clearly worn over the skin, not bare skin and not blur. Translucent beige nylon layer over natural skin tone, fine nylon knit fiber texture visible on ankle, instep, shin, and around the shoe opening. Skin details remain visible under the sheer layer: faint blue-green veins on the instep and ankle, subtle ankle bones, tendons, toe knuckle shapes, soft toe outlines, natural skin tone variation, small pores softened by nylon. Slight fabric tension around toes, ball of foot, ankle, and vamp opening; subtle matte-satin sheen following the leg curve.
```

## Pose Physics Block: Tiptoe Pump Test

```text
Pose: one foot flat on the studio floor. The other foot is in a strong tiptoe / high heel-raised pose, heel lifted high off the floor, supported mostly by the ball of the foot and toes inside the pump. The raised foot shows realistic shoe deformation from weight and flex: vamp creases at the ball-of-foot flex line, shallow horizontal compression wrinkles across the vamp, slight side bulging and compression at the metatarsal area, subtle tension along the shoe opening, realistic shadow under the lifted heel.
```

```text

Composition: camera close to floor, front three-quarter low angle from ankle down, both shoes fully framed, neutral light gray studio floor and background, soft realistic shadows, crisp commercial product photography for Korean online shop. Ultra sharp micro-details for leather grain, nylon fibers, visible skin-under-hosiery anatomy, vamp creases, toe box, shoe opening, heel shape, and floor contact.

Negative constraints: no text, no labels, no logos, no cropped shoes, no cropped heels, no distorted feet, no mismatched shoes, no extra limbs, no warped heel, no impossible ankle angle, no melted stocking texture, no bare-leg appearance, no smooth blur filter, no opaque tights, no fishnet, no dirty marks.
```
